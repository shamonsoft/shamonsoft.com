About ShamonSoft

Built by one developer with a passion for creative software.

ShamonSoft is an independent software studio focused on visual effects, customization and making Windows software that is enjoyable to use.

The idea behind ShamonSoft

ShamonSoft started from a simple habit: seeing an idea for a piece of software and wanting to build it. I am a single developer, and most of what I make starts with something I would want to use myself.

I am a vibe coder with a strong interest in visual effects, interface design and experimentation. AI is part of the development process, but the software does not get thrown together and shipped untouched. I spend a lot of time testing, changing layouts, reworking features and pushing each application until it feels like a real finished product.

I am especially drawn to software that gives people room to create. Fireworks Studio is about designing a show. Audio Visualizer is about turning sound into something visual. Cursor Studio takes a part of Windows most people never think about and turns it into something customizable.

Trust matters too. ShamonSoft software is built to be clean and straightforward. No bundled junk, no malware, no sneaky installers and no unnecessary background processes. The goal is to make software people can install, use and enjoy without wondering what else came along for the ride.

ShamonSoft is still growing, and that is part of the fun. I want it to become a home for creative desktop software that looks good, works well and gives people something a little different from the usual utility app.

Independent Windows software focused on creativity, visual effects, customization and useful tools.
