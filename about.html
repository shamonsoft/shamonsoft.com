About ShamonSoft

Creative software, built independently.

ShamonSoft is a one developer software studio focused on visual effects, customization and creative Windows applications.

The idea behind ShamonSoft

ShamonSoft started from a simple habit: seeing an idea for a piece of software and wanting to build it. I am a single developer, and most of what I create starts with something I would genuinely want to use myself.

I am a vibe coder with a strong interest in visual effects, interface design and experimentation. AI is part of how I develop, but I do not simply generate something and call it finished. I spend a lot of time testing, changing layouts, refining features and working through the details until the software feels right.

I am especially drawn to software that gives people room to create. Fireworks Studio lets you build and choreograph your own shows. Audio Visualizer turns music into a visual experience. Cursor Studio takes something as ordinary as a Windows cursor and gives you the tools to make it your own.

Trust is important. ShamonSoft software is built to be clean and straightforward. No bundled junk, no malware, no unwanted extras and no unnecessary background processes. What you download is the software you asked for, nothing more.

ShamonSoft is still growing, and that is part of what makes it exciting. I want it to become a home for creative desktop software that looks good, works well and gives people something a little different from the usual utility app.
