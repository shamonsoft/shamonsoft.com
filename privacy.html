Privacy

We don’t track you.

ShamonSoft software is built to respect your privacy. The applications do not collect or transmit your personal information or usage data.

Your files stay yours.Your projects, music, images, videos, cursor designs, fireworks shows and other project content stay on your computer. ShamonSoft does not upload or access them.

No accounts, telemetry or analytics

ShamonSoft applications do not require user accounts and do not include telemetry, analytics, advertising trackers, behavioral tracking or background data collection. ShamonSoft does not sell, rent or share personal information because the applications do not collect it.

No hidden data transmission

The applications do not send your projects, usage history or other personal data to ShamonSoft. There are no ShamonSoft cloud accounts or remote user profiles behind the applications.

Support email

If you contact support@shamonsoft.com, ShamonSoft receives only the information you choose to send in your message so that a response can be provided.

This website

ShamonSoft does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers or behavioral tracking on this website. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages, which serves the pages you request. If you choose to follow a link to a software store, social platform or another external website, that service has its own privacy practices.

If this ever changes

If a future ShamonSoft product or website feature begins collecting information, this policy will be updated to explain what is collected and why. The current ShamonSoft applications do not collect user data.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@shamonsoft.com.
